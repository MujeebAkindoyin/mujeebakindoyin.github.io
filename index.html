About Me

Mujeeb A. Akindoyin is a journalist, Mass Communication student at the University of Ilorin, and Reporter & Researcher at Platform Times. He is also a team member at OC Legend Media, Brand Ambassador and Sales & Marketing Specialist at Iblan Eyewear, and Lecturer at Deen Reach International Institute.

His work moves between reporting, research, and communication — covering politics and public affairs while studying the media systems that shape them.
